**Chinook Music Brand - Data Analysis Project**

**📌 Project Overview**

A comprehensive analysis of the Chinook Music Brand's performance, customer behavior, and strategic growth opportunities. The music database contains detailed information about artists, albums, tracks, playlists, and customers. This project aims to understand music trends, identify popular artists and top-selling albums, and analyze customer purchasing behavior to drive actionable business decisions.

**🛠️ Tools & Technologies**

1- Database Management: MySQL.

2- Cleaning and transformation : Using Power Query.

3- Data Modeling & Visualization: Power BI.

**🗄️ Data Modeling**

The database was structured using a Star Schema to optimize query performance and reporting.

Fact Table: Sales Fact.

Dimension Tables: Invoice DIM, Track DIM, Employee DIM, and Customer DIM.

**📊 Core KPIs**

1- Total Sales: $2,351.

2- Average Order Value (AOV): $5.71.

3- Total Tracks Sold: 2,240.

4- Average Track Price: $1.05.

5- Average Track Time: 393.6 seconds.

6- Average Tracks In Album: 6.5.

**💡 Key Business Insights**

1- Revenue & Employee Performance: The year 2022 was the most successful year for the store, generating $4.3K in revenue. Jane Peacock was identified as the top-performing employee.

2- Top Genres: Rock is the primary driver of the business, coming in first place as the most selling, requested, and profitable genre, followed closely by the Latin genre.

3- Market Leadership: The USA is the dominant market, generating the highest revenue at $4.7K.

4- Product Preferences: Short-timed tracks (under 1000 seconds) are the most lucrative, bringing in $19.0K in revenue. Additionally, the MPEG audio file is the preferred media type among customers.

5- Inventory Efficiency: A significant 40.41% of the track inventory remains unsold, highlighting a major area for operational improvement.

6- Top Artists & Albums: Iron Maiden is the absolute top performer in both sales and revenue, and is also the most productive artist with 213 albums. The highest-selling album overall is "Battlestar Galactica".

**🚀 Strategic Recommendations**

1- Strategic Genre Investment: Allocate resources and marketing efforts heavily towards the Rock and Latin genres, as they consistently demonstrate high sales and revenue potential.
